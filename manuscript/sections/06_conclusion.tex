\section{Conclusion}

Across the 27 EU Member States and EU27\_2020, the 2010--2022 comparison yielded 12 agreements, no resolved-class directional disagreements and 15 unresolved Member-State cases under the primary common two-sided rule. Restricting released CMUR to verified source-anchor years (B1) reduced model-based precision: intervals were wider in all 27 Member States, with a median paired B1/A width ratio of 1.858. Through 2022, most annual-change, turning and acceleration claims remained unresolved under both U and B at every tested decision threshold.

This study provides a CMUR-specific, Member-State-wide map of temporal-claim support. It shows that medium-term direction and fine-scale annual temporal claims have different evidential support under source-aware analysis. Annual publication frequency alone should not be read as equal support for every annual temporal interpretation; publication frequency and supported inferential resolution are distinct properties in this CMUR case.

The true odd-year CMUR error remains unidentified, and U and B are sensitivity conditions rather than estimates of that error distribution. Whether the same support pattern holds beyond CMUR requires replication. By distinguishing support across temporal scales, this study provides a basis for more discriminating temporal interpretation of CMUR.
